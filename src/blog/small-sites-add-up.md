---
title: "Small sites add up to a real skillset"
description: "You don't learn Eleventy from one big project. You learn it from a dozen small ones."
date: 2026-02-18
---

I didn't really understand collections, pagination, or the data cascade until I'd rebuilt small pieces of them a few times over, in different projects, for different reasons. Reading the docs once didn't do it. Reading them again after hitting the same wall a second time did.

That's basically the reason this site exists. Instead of the lesson living in five old repos I never look at again, it lives here, where I can actually find it.
